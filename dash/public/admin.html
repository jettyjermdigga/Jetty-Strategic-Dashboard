Setup

What is on the dash, and who gets it. A tile appears once it has an address, on the dash of everybody it is given to — so an unfinished one sits here harmlessly until it is ready. A section shows up because somebody has a tile in it. Getting into each tool is set up on that tool, by whoever built it. Links are bookmarks rather than tools, and everybody sees them.
