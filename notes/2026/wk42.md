## Summary

Revenue is **$11.11M against $12.24M planned — $1.13M behind, 9.3%**. Carrying
the year out on seasonality puts revenue at **$13.44M against a $14.38M plan**,
a **$939K** shortfall, and full-year net income at **−$667K**.

That net income figure has now moved three weeks running: **−$245K, −$551K,
−$667K**. The rate has slowed, but the direction has not changed.

The revenue gap widened another **$100K** this week, and **$90K of that is
the sample sale moving**, not trading. It ran this weekend in 2025, so the
plan has it in week 42; this year it follows Halloween and runs the weekend
of **7–8 November**. The revenue is coming, two or three weeks later than
the plan books it, and the week-42 gap is overstated by roughly that much
until it lands.

Costs are still not the problem, and on this week's numbers they are helping.
COGS is **$286K under plan** and labour projects **$2.76M against a $2.97M**
annual plan, **$201K under**. OpEx is **$144K over** — down from $167K last
week — and **$135K of that is Advertising**, so one line is still carrying
almost the whole overage.
