## Summary

Revenue is **$11.62M against $12.80M planned — $1.19M behind, 9.3%**. Carrying
the year out on seasonality puts revenue at **$13.42M against a $14.38M plan**,
a **$955K** shortfall, and full-year net income at **−$532K**.

That net income figure had moved the wrong way three weeks running — **−$245K,
−$551K, −$667K**. Week 44 is the first reversal: **−$532K**, a **$135K**
improvement. None of it came from revenue, which is flat on trend. All of it
came from cost.

The sample sale landed as expected. Mobile Store & Tent was **−$75K** at week
42 and is **+$27K** now, a **$102K** swing, slightly better than the ~$90K
called last week. But the gap still widened **$58K** over the two weeks,
because **INK got $96K worse and Wholesale $34K worse** in the same period and
ate the whole thing.

Costs continue to help. COGS is **$364K under plan**, up from $286K under at
week 42. OpEx is **$92K over**, down from $144K. Labour projects **$2.74M
against a $2.97M** annual plan, **$223K under**.

Every tab is keyed through week 44 — A/R, Inventory and Sell-through came in
after the first read of this week, and both of them changed the picture.

## Labor

Labour is where this week's real finding is. Split by side:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Brand | $1,308K | $1,518K | −$210K | 86.2% |

**Brand revenue is running at 94% of plan and cut labour to 86%. INK revenue
is running at 79% of plan and cut labour to 99%.** Brand flexed down faster
than its sales fell; INK did not flex at all. That is the whole of INK's
margin problem — it is not pricing and it is not input cost, which came down
faster than revenue did. It is that a 20% volume drop was carried with a full
crew.

INK sales. Latest payroll (5 Nov) was **$106,126** with **50.85 OT hours**.

## What would change the read

- **INK's cost base.** A 20% volume shortfall carried at 99% of planned
  labour is the clearest single decision in these numbers. Brand already made
  the other choice. This is worth more than anything else on the list.
- **Collection.** $1.23M of A/R against $152K of cash. Coverage of CNS
  payables improved to 1.25x this week, which is the right direction — but
  the improvement came from billing more, not from collecting more, and only
  one of those turns into cash. Still the only source that does not touch the
  line.
- **The $61.6K cash gap**, and whether Ramp explains it. If it does not, the
  week-44 balance is overstated.
- **$2.62M on order, 95% of it SU27 and Starboard.** Next year's buy is the
  next call on this year's cash, and it is already committed.
- **Advertising at $128K over**, projecting $653K against a $525K plan, while
  revenue runs $1.19M under.
- Whether the rest of the sample sale lands in week 45, and whether Wholesale
  and INK stabilise in Q4 or keep sliding. Over the last two weeks they took
  $130K more out of the gap than the sample sale put back.
