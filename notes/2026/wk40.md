## Where week 40 leaves us

Revenue is **$10.85M against a plan of $11.82M — $969K behind, or 8.2%**. That gap
is the shape of the whole year rather than a recent slip.

Six of seven channels are below plan, and the exception is a real one:

- **Jettylife.com (DTC): $1.77M against $1.42M planned, +25.3%**
- Wholesale: −$527K (−9.2%)
- Screen printing (INK): −$523K (−19.5%)
- Flagship Store: −$213K (−24.0%)
- Long Branch: −$141K (−20.7%)
- Mobile Store & Tent: +$5K (+1.5%), essentially level

## Where I would push back

COGS is $459K under plan, and that reads as a win. Most of it is arithmetic: you
bought and made less because you sold less. The rate says something different.

- COGS + Labor + Shipping as a share of revenue: **77.0% actual against 74.6% planned — 2.4 points worse**
- Strip labor out, which is semi-fixed and flattered by a revenue miss, and it is still **58.9% against 56.2%**

So the dollars are favourable and the margin is not. Those 2.4 points are worth
about **$263K** on the revenue actually booked. A cost line under plan while its
rate drifts is the easiest thing on this page to misread.

OpEx is the clearer problem, and the instinct to watch it is right — more right
than the dollar figure suggests. It is $124K over plan, but **28.1% of revenue
against 24.8% planned, 3.4 points**. Fixed cost against a revenue miss hurts twice.

## The number that matters

Net income year to date is **−$793K against a planned −$127K**. The full-year
projection has moved from a planned **+$196K profit to a projected −$469K loss** —
a **$665K swing**.

## On cash, and why it looks better than it is

The bank balance recovered to $161K. The reason is not revenue: **cash out is
$2.6M under plan while cash in is only $736K under**. Cash is being held by not
spending it, and since OpEx is *over* plan, the underspend is in buying.

That is borrowed time if it means thin inventory through the holiday weeks. The
Inventory tab is still on week 39, so what is actually on hand is the one thing
this note cannot see.

## What I would watch down the stretch

- **Margin rate, not margin dollars.** Volume is not coming to fix the rate.
- **OpEx in absolute dollars.** At 28% of revenue there is no volume arriving to absorb it.
- **DTC.** The only channel beating plan, and beating it by a quarter. Worth knowing exactly why, because nothing else is.
